# Epic 7: Complete a Replayable Encounter-Driven Level

The player can enter a loaded level, traverse meaningful routes, resolve required and optional encounters, collect rewards, use checkpoints, die, restart, understand state through the HUD and audio, adjust settings, and complete a non-boss encounter sequence without stale state.

**Primary functional requirements:** FR46, FR47, FR48, FR49, FR50, FR51, FR52, FR53, FR57, FR58
