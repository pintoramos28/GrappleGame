# Story 8.12: Complete and Replay the Last Garden Slice

As a player,
I want to complete and replay the full Last Garden boss slice as one coherent experience,
So that the game's traversal-combat promise is proven from level entry through Garden Heart victory, reward, and exit.

**Acceptance Criteria:**

1. **Declare the final M4 integration outcome**

    **Given** Stories 8.1 through 8.11 have produced their approved design, gameplay, flow, presentation, reward, exit, and performance evidence
    **When** the final M4 gate is inspected
    **Then** a player can begin from safe UI, load the Last Garden, traverse its approach, complete the required approach encounter, activate the boss checkpoint, fight and defeat the Garden Heart through traversal-created openings, claim the boss reward, exit, and begin a fresh replay
    **And** named end-to-end journeys cover player-facing comprehension, deliberate failure and recovery, boss mastery, reward and exit, repeatability, cleanup, rate equivalence, release export, performance prerequisite, and retained evidence
    **And** the story validates the frozen candidate rather than inventing missing features or silently repairing prerequisites
    **And** final media, narrative implementation, persistent RPG progression, full-world content, production balance, and a research report remain outside this story.

2. **Require every Epic 8 prerequisite to pass**

    **Given** final M4 validation is about to run
    **When** prerequisite evidence is checked
    **Then** the approved Garden Heart design, Last Garden shell, approach encounter, core boss, weak points, primary cycle, phase escalation, presentation, encounter and checkpoint flow, reward and exit, and M4 performance gate all have passing current-version results
    **And** Story 6.8's selected production subset and the inherited Epic 1 through Epic 7 contracts match the candidate build
    **And** failed, stale, provisional where a final pass is required, superseded, or unapproved evidence prevents sign-off
    **And** material repair remains separately scoped and reverified rather than being hidden in the final record.

3. **Freeze one versioned M4 candidate**

    **Given** the integration candidate is assembled
    **When** its handoff manifest is recorded
    **Then** it identifies application build and source revision, engine and physics configuration, Last Garden level and manifest, approach encounter and role definitions, selected mechanic versions, Garden Heart and weak-point definitions, phase and action definitions, HUD and presentation specification, cue definitions, checkpoint, reward and exit definitions, diagnostic schema, benchmark result, and dependency versions
    **And** every required runtime resource is reachable through validated application, level, encounter, and boss manifests
    **And** mutable run, boss, player, checkpoint, reward, UI, and audio state remains outside those shared definitions
    **And** changing a listed dependency after evidence collection invalidates or deliberately versions affected results.

4. **Start from normal safe application flow**

    **Given** the Windows application launches with no active level
    **When** either named journey begins
    **Then** the resident safe menu is usable with current supported preferences and keyboard-and-mouse bindings
    **And** selecting the Last Garden validation slice requests its stable level ID through the catalog and controlled loading boundary
    **And** all required content becomes resident before one fresh level session and player activate
    **And** no editor launch, direct scene path, diagnostic command, retained prior run, or disposable tutorial level is required.

5. **Traverse the authored Last Garden approach**

    **Given** one fresh Last Garden session is active
    **When** the player moves from entry to the approach encounter and boss entrance
    **Then** production ground, air, jump, grapple, maximum-length, release-momentum, wall, attack, camera, input, and ordinary recovery contracts remain available through the authored low, high, lateral, and recovery paths
    **And** route choice changes exposure, target access, approach, or recovery value under the selected pressure
    **And** ordinary mistakes permit local recovery while severe authored failure reaches the approved recovery flow
    **And** no route depends on an unapproved target, hidden anchor, forced locomotion state, diagnostic teleport, or old tutorial geometry.

6. **Complete the selected approach encounter coherently**

    **Given** the player crosses the approach commitment threshold
    **When** Story 8.3's approved supporting-role composition activates
    **Then** only roles and mechanics advanced by Story 6.8 and allocated by Story 8.1 participate
    **And** their telegraphs, route effects, target priorities, counters, ordinary recovery, source death, and completion remain understandable with available or primitive presentation
    **And** the player can use at least two meaningful route or target responses where the approved composition supports them
    **And** encounter completion clears pressure, opens onward progression, and permits safe travel to the boss checkpoint exactly once.

7. **Activate the boss checkpoint and encounter cleanly**

    **Given** the approach prerequisite is committed
    **When** the player reaches the boss checkpoint and crosses the arena commitment threshold
    **Then** the checkpoint captures its approved safe in-memory state and one transactional Garden Heart run becomes active
    **And** the gate closes safely, the player remains in a valid start or recovery state, and the initial boss phase begins only after complete readiness
    **And** boss, weak points, actions, selected pressure, HUD, audio, presentation, and arena state share the current level-session and encounter-run identities
    **And** duplicate crossings, partial readiness, stale prerequisites, or inert stand-ins cannot create another boss.

8. **Demonstrate one readable primary attack-response cycle**

    **Given** the Garden Heart is active in its approved initial context
    **When** the primary cycle executes
    **Then** the player can perceive its source, windup, affected space or target, lock behavior, active consequence, primary response, ordinary failure consequence, recovery, counter result, vulnerability relationship, and next-cycle readiness
    **And** the successful response can be performed through normal traversal and combat controls
    **And** a failed response does not make the remaining fight permanently unwinnable
    **And** diagnostics, raw timers, internal IDs, and animation or audio completion are not required.

9. **Turn traversal into measurable offense**

    **Given** the player responds successfully to the approved opening setup
    **When** the mapped weak point becomes actionable
    **Then** the player can reach and attack it through the intended velocity, altitude, trajectory, wall, grapple, timing, or route relationship
    **And** movement context from the authored capture phase and current impact context determine the documented outcome without confusing approach history with current collision facts
    **And** the baseline and optional two-second diagnostic melee active-window variants remain available for the previously approved feel comparison without changing the shipping candidate silently
    **And** stationary, wrong-angle, closed-window, stale, duplicate, or wrong-target attacks do not receive the traversal-created benefit.

10. **Experience every approved boss phase and player question**

    **Given** accepted damage and counter results advance the fight
    **When** the Garden Heart moves through its complete phase graph
    **Then** each phase asks the Story 8.1-approved route, altitude, timing, trajectory, observation, target-priority, counter-sequence, or recovery question
    **And** only selected pressures and approved overlaps occur
    **And** low, high, lateral, and required recovery paths remain useful within each phase's explicit rules
    **And** escalation remains distinct through gameplay rather than hidden damage inflation, color changes, or health alone.

11. **Preserve the full approved combat contract**

    **Given** the player and Garden Heart attack, move, collide, take damage, recover, transition, or die
    **When** authoritative outcomes resolve
    **Then** simulation-owned lifecycles, one motor commit, immutable definitions, owner-local state, MovementCombatContext, current ImpactContext, damage snapshots, weak-point facts, health, AI intent, run scope, and cleanup follow their established contracts
    **And** the wind mechanic's approved ten-metre-per-second contribution limit remains a bounded component contribution rather than a hard cap on total player velocity wherever that selected mechanic appears
    **And** no animation, audio, VFX, HUD, diagnostic, or scene-tree order creates a gameplay phase or result
    **And** every execution and damage or death occurrence reaches one idempotent terminal result.

12. **Understand the fight through player-facing presentation**

    **Given** development diagnostics are hidden
    **When** boss identity, health, phases, actions, affected spaces, weak points, counters, player health, grapple state, objective state, death, checkpoint, reward, and exit change
    **Then** the approved HUD, world cues, fallback or available model and animation presentation, semantic audio, VFX, camera, and environment communicate each required state
    **And** critical distinctions do not rely on color alone and central traversal space remains readable
    **And** the complete slice remains playable when optional final-quality media is absent and its fallback is used
    **And** future media adapters can be replaced without altering the validated gameplay trace.

13. **Keep pause, preferences, and input safe during the boss slice**

    **Given** the player pauses during representative approach, boss windup, active pressure, phase transition, and post-defeat states
    **When** they resume, change a supported presentation or audio preference, or use one valid remapped keyboard-and-mouse layout
    **Then** simulation remains suspended until owner-controlled resume and continues without catch-up, duplicated phases, stale input, or changed outcomes
    **And** HUD scale, reticle options, high contrast, audio categories, sensitivity, and bindings affect only their approved consumers
    **And** cancelled or invalid setting and binding changes do not leak into gameplay
    **And** the slice remains completable after restoring factory bindings and baseline preferences.

14. **Recover from player death without stale boss state**

    **Given** the player dies during an approved boss action, vulnerability window, or phase transition
    **When** each recovery choice approved for that state is exercised
    **Then** Restart Encounter and Reload Checkpoint follow Story 8.9's recovery matrix and restore the intended player, prerequisite, gate, arena, and objective state
    **And** every new attempt creates a fresh Garden Heart with baseline health, initial phase, action history, weak points, deterministic occurrence, HUD, audio, and presentation
    **And** no prior action, projectile, hazard, effect, surface mutation, weak-point result, damage, audio, or callback can affect it
    **And** retry friction, checkpoint placement, and recovered player readiness are assessed through normal controls.

15. **Defeat the Garden Heart through readable mastery**

    **Given** the player completes enough legitimate response and opening cycles
    **When** accepted player attacks reduce current boss health to zero
    **Then** Garden Heart death, participant terminal state, encounter completion, and boss LevelObjective completion each commit exactly once through their owners
    **And** the victory trace contains the required traversal-created openings rather than unrestricted health attrition
    **And** hostile pressure and temporary arena state clean up before the safe post-defeat route becomes usable
    **And** stale hits, closed weak points, missing geometry, presentation, or direct reward access cannot produce victory.

16. **Claim exactly one intended boss reward**

    **Given** the current boss objective is complete
    **When** the player reaches and collects the Story 8.10 reward
    **Then** one immutable logical grant adds exactly the explicitly approved Garden Heart coin amount to the current-session ledger and coin total
    **And** the pickup, HUD, and semantic audio communicate one accepted collection
    **And** duplicate overlap, rollback, replayed boss completion, or stale grant work cannot award it again in the same level session
    **And** the result does not imply inventory, saved currency, or account progression.

17. **Exit through controlled completed-level flow**

    **Given** boss objective and reward collection are committed
    **When** the current player crosses the authored exit threshold
    **Then** LevelController accepts one exit occurrence and GameFlowController invalidates and tears down the Last Garden before publishing the approved completion or safe-menu state
    **And** attempting exit before collection gives the approved unavailable response without partial transition
    **And** UI and audio unbind, input resets, and all old level authority ends through established flow
    **And** duplicate or stale threshold observations cannot begin another exit or preserve old gameplay.

18. **Begin a genuinely fresh replay**

    **Given** the completed-slice destination offers the approved Replay action
    **When** the player selects it
    **Then** application flow loads a new Last Garden session through the controlled boundary rather than reactivating or scrubbing the old level
    **And** new player, approach run, checkpoint, boss run, boss occurrence, reward ledger, pickup, objective, HUD, audio, presentation, and exit identities are allocated
    **And** player and Garden Heart health, phases, gates, arena state, reward availability, exit eligibility, and current-session coins begin from authored baseline
    **And** application-owned settings and valid bindings persist only according to their established policies.

19. **Recover safely from representative integration failures**

    **Given** controlled fixtures inject missing Last Garden or boss dependencies, approach activation failure, Garden Heart construction failure, gate failure, invalid checkpoint state, reward application failure, exit-transition failure, presentation failure, audio failure, or teardown failure
    **When** the responsible owner resolves the problem
    **Then** it produces one typed bounded result and no partial success owned by another domain
    **And** healthy state is preserved where the transaction permits it, otherwise flow reaches approved recovery or safe resident UI
    **And** optional media failure uses its readable fallback while required gameplay failure blocks activation or completion
    **And** retry starts through a new authorized request rather than continuing terminal work indefinitely.

20. **Preserve exactly-once outcomes across the complete slice**

    **Given** duplicate, simultaneous, reordered, delayed, or stale facts occur at loading, approach, checkpoint, boss activation, actions, counters, weak points, phase transitions, damage, death, recovery, objective, reward, exit, and replay boundaries
    **When** their owners resolve them
    **Then** each level session, encounter run, execution, transition, damage result, death, checkpoint transaction, objective, grant, collection, exit, and application request reaches no more than its declared one terminal result
    **And** callback, signal, scene-tree, UI, render, audio, or asynchronous resource-completion order cannot create another authority
    **And** stable scope and occurrence identities reject prior work
    **And** expected duplicate and stale handling remains bounded.

21. **Remove every old scoped object between attempts and sessions**

    **Given** a journey includes approach restart, boss death, encounter restart, checkpoint reload, exit, return to menu, replay, and level replacement
    **When** each lifetime boundary completes
    **Then** scoped participants, bosses, weak points, actions, projectiles, telegraphs, hazards, obstacles, links, statuses, surface mutations, pickups, timers, audio emitters, VFX, camera requests, HUD bindings, input latches, deferred work, and signal connections terminate exactly once or idempotently
    **And** registries and runtime roots report only the expected current-session objects or zero after teardown
    **And** no retained node reference keeps an old level, player, enemy, boss, pickup, or presenter alive
    **And** immutable definition fingerprints remain unchanged through every attempt and replay.

22. **Complete Journey A: mastery, victory, reward, exit, and replay**

    **Given** the application starts at safe menu with factory bindings, baseline preferences, and diagnostics hidden
    **When** another developer follows the named Journey A procedure
    **Then** they load the Last Garden, use at least two meaningful approach routes, complete the selected approach encounter, activate the boss checkpoint, enter the arena, demonstrate the primary response, open and attack every required weak-point type, experience each phase and approved overlap, and defeat the Garden Heart
    **And** they collect exactly the approved boss coin reward, verify the exit unlocks only afterward, exit through normal flow, select Replay, and reach a fresh Last Garden session
    **And** every required state and decision is understandable through level geometry, HUD, world cues, audio, and fallback or available presentation
    **And** objective logs and post-run scope assertions confirm one valid completion, one reward, one exit, and complete old-session teardown.

23. **Complete Journey B: failure, both recoveries, and eventual victory**

    **Given** the application starts a fresh Last Garden session with one valid custom binding and an approved accessibility or presentation preference changed
    **When** another developer follows the named Journey B procedure with diagnostics hidden
    **Then** they complete the approach, activate the boss checkpoint, deliberately fail the primary response and recover ordinarily, die during a representative active pressure and use Restart Encounter, then exercise Reload Checkpoint at the approved second recovery point
    **And** each retry begins with the exact intended checkpoint and fresh baseline boss state while settings and bindings remain correct
    **And** they pause and resume during a later phase, defeat the Garden Heart, test one premature exit observation, collect the reward once, exit, and return to safe menu
    **And** no recovery, repeated boss completion, reward contact, or exit crossing duplicates health, phase, objectives, coins, presentation, audio, or application transitions.

24. **Run player-facing comprehension before diagnostics**

    **Given** each named journey has a primary play pass
    **When** that pass begins
    **Then** development overlays, world drawing, invulnerability, AI pause, diagnostic selection, reset or reload shortcuts, forced phase controls, and automatic capture are disabled
    **And** the tester uses only normal menu, gameplay, pause, settings, death, recovery, reward, exit, and replay interactions
    **And** inability to identify a threat, response, opening, objective, reward, exit, or recovery cannot be excused by debug-only information
    **And** diagnostics may be enabled only in a later audit to explain already observed behavior.

25. **Assess mastery rather than health attrition**

    **Given** the player-facing journeys and additional playtest attempts are complete
    **When** boss quality observations are reviewed
    **Then** evidence records whether players understand the attack-response loop, use traversal to create openings, recognize phase questions, recover from ordinary mistakes, and attribute victory to learned decisions
    **And** successful runs record legitimate vulnerability and damage traces sufficient to distinguish mastery from unrestricted attrition
    **And** subjective clarity, difficulty, pacing, fatigue, spectacle, fairness, satisfaction, and desire to replay remain labelled as human judgments
    **And** a failed core comprehension or mastery condition remains visible for separately approved correction rather than being averaged away.

26. **Audit the complete slice with bounded diagnostics**

    **Given** player-facing passes finish
    **When** a diagnostic audit repeats critical boundaries
    **Then** maintained owner data confirms player motor and contact, grapple, attacks, movement and impact contexts, Garden Heart phases and actions, weak points, AI selection, encounter and level scope, checkpoint, objective, reward, audio, presentation, performance, exit, and replay identities agree with observed play
    **And** finite development commands are exercised only in the specifically documented command audit
    **And** one bounded local capture records the frozen candidate's representative boss state and complete cleanup evidence
    **And** enabling diagnostics cannot change selection, timing, damage, recovery, reward, exit, or pass status.

27. **Verify the integrated slice automatically**

    **Given** permanent contract suites and an M4 end-to-end smoke harness run against the frozen candidate
    **When** they exercise startup, loading, approach routes and encounter, boss checkpoint, activation, primary cycle, selected mechanics, every phase, counters, weak points, damage and death, player death and approved recovery, HUD and audio, pause and preferences, reward, premature and accepted exit, teardown, replay, injected failures, stale work, and repeated sessions
    **Then** all required contracts remain passing and each level session reaches at most one valid boss objective, reward collection, and exit
    **And** no partial load, duplicate authority, stale mutation, escaped transient, retained source, mismatched presentation, or changed immutable definition survives
    **And** tests use deterministic owner state and scenario boundaries rather than arbitrary sleeps, pixel-perfect gameplay screenshots, private node paths, or animation callbacks
    **And** human play remains required for route value, attack readability, counter comprehension, control comfort, sound clarity, boss quality, and overall feel.

28. **Preserve real-time behavior at both physics rates**

    **Given** equivalent deterministic Journey A boundaries and focused Journey B recovery cases run at shipping 60 Hz and diagnostic 120 Hz
    **When** authoritative traces are compared
    **Then** movement and attack timing, selected actions, pressure geometry, counter results, weak-point openings, damage, phase order, checkpoint state, recovery, objective, reward amount, exit, cleanup, and terminal results remain equivalent within documented tolerance
    **And** high-speed traversal, melee, grapple, and threshold queries remain reliable without rate-dependent tunnelling
    **And** presentation sample density may differ without changing authoritative outcomes
    **And** any rate-sensitive gameplay divergence blocks Epic 8 completion.

29. **Require the approved M4 performance result**

    **Given** Story 8.11 produced its decision-grade result for the frozen candidate
    **When** final Epic 8 completion is evaluated
    **Then** the actual selected minimum-spec Windows PC has a passing result for the approved 1920-by-1080 stable-60 criteria
    **And** selected roles, boss phases, legal worst-case overlap, presentation, reward, exit, build, definitions, and settings match the final candidate
    **And** diagnostics are disabled and combat-critical synchronous loading is absent in the qualifying run
    **And** proxy hardware, provisional status, or a result from superseded content cannot satisfy the gate.

30. **Confirm the release-like Windows build is complete**

    **Given** the frozen candidate is exported for Windows
    **When** Journeys A and B receive their required export smoke coverage
    **Then** application bootstrap, Forward+ rendering, Jolt physics, LimboAI, native dependencies, fonts, settings, bindings, fallback visuals, HUD, audio, level loading, approach, boss, reward, exit, teardown, menu, and replay function without editor-only content
    **And** debug-only commands, forced states, verbose diagnostics, and fixture shortcuts are absent or disabled
    **And** the current disposable tutorial level is not required by startup, player systems, prompts, loading, or Last Garden gameplay
    **And** any export-only failure blocks sign-off.

31. **Retain one complete M4 evidence package**

    **Given** objective and human-review checks finish
    **When** the final package is assembled
    **Then** it records frozen candidate versions, prerequisites, Journey A and B procedures and results, comprehension and mastery observations, failure matrix, exactly-once assertions, cleanup and repeatability counts, 60/120 comparison, Windows export smoke, minimum-spec result, diagnostic capture reference, requirement traceability, accepted limitations, and unresolved risks
    **And** objective results remain separate from subjective route, readability, audio, control, pacing, difficulty, spectacle, satisfaction, and replay observations
    **And** failures and limitations remain visible rather than being rewritten as passes
    **And** another developer can reproduce the result without the excluded research report or undocumented project knowledge.

32. **Trace the final slice requirements to evidence**

    **Given** the final traceability matrix is reviewed
    **When** FR1 and FR54 through FR56 plus applicable NFR and architecture requirements are mapped
    **Then** each requirement identifies its primary source story, integrated journey step, objective evidence, human evidence where required, candidate version, and current status
    **And** authored level progression, selected-role provenance, boss cycle, phases, traversal opening, impact-based damage, death, objective, reward, exit, replay, HUD, audio, timing, ownership, cleanup, performance, export, accessibility, and release-exclusion rules have no unexplained gap
    **And** a missing, failed, stale, or falsely claimed mapping blocks Epic 8 completion
    **And** traceability does not claim deferred narrative, persistent RPG, final-media, tutorial, or complete-world outcomes.

33. **Keep the final story bounded to integration and sign-off**

    **Given** Story 8.12 is reviewed for completion
    **When** its delivered scope is enumerated
    **Then** it contains the frozen M4 candidate, two named player-facing journeys, mastery review, recovery and failure audit, diagnostic audit, automated smoke, rate comparison, performance and export prerequisites, traceability, and final evidence package
    **And** it does not create new roles or mechanics, change the selected subset, redesign the boss, invent a reward value, add persistent inventory or saving, recreate the tutorial, produce final art, animation, VFX, or audio, add narrative, or introduce speculative optimization
    **And** passing it completes the M0-M4 Last Garden validation slice and proves the replayable boss-level loop defined by this epic.
