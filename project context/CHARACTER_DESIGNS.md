## 1. The Protagonist ("The Relic Explorer")

### Profile & Anatomy
* **Age:** 17 years old (one month prior to the village's mandatory role "Choosing").
* **Physique:** Athletic, wiry, and agile runner/climber build. Built for high-velocity acrobatics and spatial problem-solving rather than brute force.
* **Facial Features & Expression:** Determined, observant, and inquisitive. Windswept dark brown hair ruffled by high speeds.
* **Origin:** A remote highland farming settlement founded by Severance refugees who fear strange mushrooms and live in low-tech isolation.

### The Pathfinder Traversal Suit
* **Origin:** Discovered inside an ancient expedition pack beside a preserved dead Giant beneath a waterfall cavern. Relic technology from humanity's **Golden Age of Exploration** (~30 years of joint Human-Giant expeditions).
* **Base Layer:** Slate-grey (`#1E232A`) and olive-green (`#343D33`) high-durability flexible ballistic weave.
* **Kinetic Plating:** Articulated knee, shin, and elbow guards designed for high-velocity wall impacts and vertical landings.
* **Pathfinder Insignia:** A golden winged-compass emblem (`#D4AF37`) embossed on the upper chest plate—the ancient symbol recognized by the Giant elder in World 4.

## 2. The Electromagnetic Grapple Harness System

In accordance with [HISTORY_AND_FACTIONS.md](HISTORY_AND_FACTIONS.md#the-golden-age-of-exploration), humanity’s defining mastery was **electricity, electromagnetics, and precision instrumentation**. The harness is a cutting-edge electromagnetic traversal system rather than a mechanical winch.

### The Single Hexagonal Anchor ("Hex-Anchor")
* **Singular Architecture:** Exactly **one** panel exists. No ammunition counter or expendable magazines.
* **Dimensions & Materials:** Palm-sized, symmetrical 9cm hexagonal induction tile (`#182029` alloy with `#00F0FF` cyan core diode).
* **Docked State:** Sits seated flush into a dedicated receiver dock atop the right forearm gauntlet, pulsing in a low-power standby mode.
* **Surface Adhesion:** High-frequency electromagnetic induction pads and micro-resonance pins allow instantaneous, flush clamping to any surface (Giant basalt masonry, rusted metal aqueducts, or living fungal stipes).

### The Cyan Phosphorescent Plasma Link
* **Tether Composition:** No physical rope, cable, or hook. A focused, high-energy **electromagnetic plasma beam** enveloped in spiral magnetic field rings.
* **Spectral Frequency:** Emits an intense **cyan plasma glow** (`#00F0FF` / `#18E5BE`) tuned to the exact **phosphorescence of the native fungal ecology**, harmonizing with planetary fungal resonance.
* **Operational Cycle:**
  1. *Fire:* Ejected from the gauntlet rail-accelerator with an electromagnetic snap.
  2. *Anchor & Connect:* Clamps flush to target; cyan plasma beam ignites instantly.
  3. *Zip-Pull / Wall-Stick:* Pulls the protagonist along the magnetic vector toward the anchor.
  4. *Auto-Recall:* Releasing the input inverts magnetic polarity; the Hex-Anchor dislodges from the wall and whips back into the forearm dock with a tactile magnetic click.

### Torso Rig & Lumbar Capacitor Core
* **Lumbar Capacitor Pack:** Replaces bulky cable winches with a flat, contoured electromagnetic pulse capacitor and cooling heat sinks located at the player's center of gravity.
* **Superconducting Load-Bearing Webbing:** Harness straps across the chest, shoulders, and thighs are interwoven with conductive micro-filaments that distribute deceleration g-forces across the wearer's whole body.

## 3. Native Humanoid Species — The Lumenari (World 1)

### Biology & Symbiotic Morphology
* **Anatomy:** Graceful, slender humanoids living in profound biological symbiosis with the indigenous fungal network of *The Last Garden*.
* **Epidermal Features:** Smooth skin adorned with iridescent lichen patterns and subcutaneous mycelial veins that pulse with golden-turquoise bioluminescence.
* **Cranial Crests:** Living antlers and bioluminescent fungal frills (resembling glowing shelf caps and delicate filigree) sprout from their brows, serving as sensory organs sensitive to airborne spores and planetary resonance.

### Attire & Culture
* **Costume:** Layered nomadic robes woven from fibrous plant silks, dried moss felts, and living tendrils. Decorated with dried seedpods and glowing mycelial threadwork.
* **Implements:** Carry slender gardening crooks and seed vessels, using gentle cultivation rather than industrial exploitation.
* **Narrative States:**
  * *Friendly/Neutral:* Garden caretakers who show players non-violent routes through the fungal canopy.
  * *Corrupted (Bloombound Hunter):* Aggressive variants armed with thorn spears and tracking spore detonators.
